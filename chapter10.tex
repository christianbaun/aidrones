\renewcommand{\deutschertitel}{GPS-basierte Positionsbestimmung}
\renewcommand{\englischertitel}{GPS-Based Position Estimation}
\makroabschnitt
\label{GpsBasedPosition}

Drones primarily find their way using Global Navigation Satellite Systems (GNSS), which is the broad term for satellite networks like GPS. These satellites act as a "map in the sky" that allows the drone to pinpoint its exact location on Earth (its latitude, longitude, and height). Once the drone knows exactly where it is on the map, it can perform smart tasks automatically: Position Hold, Waypoints, Return to Home
